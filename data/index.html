Badge hotspot
1 to 32 characters. Saving restarts the hotspot under the new name.
Leave it empty and anyone can join without a password. If you set one, it needs 8 to 63 letters or numbers.
Hide the hotspot It will not show up in the list of networks on your phone. You will have to type the name in by hand.
Heads up: write the name down exactly as it appears above. If your phone forgets this network and you no longer remember the name, you will not get back in over Wi-Fi: you would have to plug the badge in over USB.
Turn the hotspot on The badge makes its own network. Turn this off to have it join your Wi-Fi instead.
Heads up: turning this off disconnects this page. The badge will try to join your Wi-Fi. If it cannot, plug it in over USB and press a to have it make its own network again.
Join your Wi-Fi
You have not saved a network yet. Once it connects, open http://SantaMuerte.local/ or whatever address shows up here.
Leave it empty if the network is open. The password is saved on the badge and needs 8 to 63 letters or numbers.
